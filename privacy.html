Privacy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, and your builds never leave your browser unless you share the link.

Your builds

Your build is stored only in the page address, after the "#". Browsers never send that part to a server, so the site does not see or store your builds.

Cookies, storage and analytics

The site sets no cookies, does not use your browser's storage, and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. As the site owner I can only see aggregated traffic numbers (for example total visits), not individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Fonts and other resources

All fonts and images are served from this site itself. Nothing is loaded from other websites.

Contacting me

Feedback goes through GitHub Issues, which are public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement. Don't post anything there you want to keep private.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data exists about you and ask for it to be deleted. This site stores no personal data itself, so there is usually nothing to delete, but you can always ask via the Support page.
