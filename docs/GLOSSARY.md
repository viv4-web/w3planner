# Glossary (v29): data, build and file formats

The Glossary screen copies the game's: Bestiary, Tutorial, Characters, Books (Crafting is "in game only"). Its text and pictures are **game content** and are handled
like the game art: they live in the private art folder `~/work/w3planner-art/glossary/`, are **never committed**, and `tools/build.py` copies them into the live build
(`--variant game`). The public and offline builds contain none of it; the screen then says "Glossary content is not included in this build." The repo holds the code, the
tool, this document and a tiny FAKE fixture (`tests/fixtures/glossary`, made by `tests/make_glossary_fixture.py`) the tests build the site with.

## Making the content
`python tools/build_glossary.py --out ~/work/w3planner-art/glossary` (add `--update-hashes` to append the SHA-1 of every picture to `tools/game-art-hashes.txt`, the guard against committing game art; `--check` verifies counts).
Inputs (read-only): `~/incoming/w3planner-journal.zip` (every `.journal` file of every bundle, made on Vivek's PC by `tools/pc/extract_journal_on_pc.py`), `~/incoming/gamedata/en.w3strings`,
`~/incoming/gamedata/bundles/*.bundle` + `dlc-xml/` + `inventory/` (item XML and icons, for the books), and the picture zips made on the PC with `tools/pc/extract_on_pc.py --list tools/pc/glossary-images-<tab>.txt`
(`glossary-bestiary.zip`, `-characters`, `-tutorials`, `-books`, `w3-glossary-paintings.zip`; DDS, converted here to WebP q80 with alpha). The lists in `tools/pc/glossary-images-*.txt` were built from the
texture index in the journal zip (`texture-index.txt`), so every name resolves. `tools/extract/cr2w.py` reads the journals. It prints the counts and what it dropped.

## Where the game keeps it (all in `startup.bundle` unless noted)
`gameplay\journal\bestiary\*.journal` (103 files), `characters\` (88), `tutorial\` (681); Hearts of Stone `dlc\ep1\journal\...` and Blood and Wine `dlc\bob\journal\...` (in `ep1.bundle` and `bob.bundle`), `dlc0.bundle` has one more creature. Books are NOT journals: they are items tagged
`ReadableItem` (and `Painting`) in the item XML; the body is the string `<localisation_key_name>_text`. A journal entry has `name` (a number into `en.w3strings`), `image`, description entries, and for creatures `itemsUsedAgainstCreature`.
Pictures (`texture.cache`, `gameplay\gui_new\...`): creature `textures\journal\bestiary\<image>` (654 x 727, RGBA), character `textures\journal\characters\<image>` (654 x 727), tutorial `textures\glossary\tutorials\` (60 files), paintings `icons\inventory\paintings\<id>`, book icons `icons\inventory\...`.

## Rules (decisions by Vivek, 2026-10-02)
- Everything is shown: all entries, all stages, no met or unmet, no level, no spoiler hiding.
- **Stage order**: the bestiary menu inserts each active description before the first one with a greater `order` (`glossaryBestiaryMenu.ws`, `GetDescription`): ascending `order`, ties in child order. (The spike's note that the `children` array defines it was wrong for the bestiary; characters use child order, `glossaryCharacterMenu.ws`.)
- **Bestiary**: 129 creatures (138 in the files; 9 stubs have no name or text and are dropped: Bies, Czart, Czart1, Fiend, Leshy, Leszy, Silvan, Silvan1, and `q704: The Big Bad Wolf`, whose strings are missing from the game's string file). Grouped by the game's categories (Beasts, Cursed Ones, Draconids, Elementa, Hybrids, Insectoids, Necrophages, Ogroids, Relicts, Specters, Vampires); a DLC creature joins the base group its virtual group file links to (`linkedObjectPath`). A to Z inside a group. **Susceptibility**: `itemsUsedAgainstCreature` names; one that is an id in `data/consumables.json` is a link (`{"k":"item","id","name"}`: a potion or decoction opens the Potion 1 panel, a bomb the Bomb 1 panel, with the card selected; an oil opens the Oil row of the sword it fits, highlighted), the five signs, `Signs Enhancement` (a quest potion left out of our data) and `Chain Proto` (in no item XML) are plain labels.
- **Characters**: 116, grouped Base game, Hearts of Stone, Blood and Wine; all have a real portrait.
- **Tutorial**: 508 entries, grouped by the game's categories (29: Additional Content, Alchemy, Boats, Bombs, Character, Ciri, Climbing, Combat, Console Features, Dialogue, Fast Travel, Gwent, Horses, HUD, Interacting, Inventory, Items, Journal, Living World, Meditation, Mixtures, Movement, Mutagens, Mutations, Oils, Signs, Swimming, Weapons, Witcher Senses; "Other" holds the 18 whose group is not named), each group A to Z and foldable like the Bestiary's. 627 journal entries minus 19 with no name or no text (Gwent tutorials and leftovers) = 608, then 100 platform variants folded into the PC entry (suffix `_pad`, `_ps4`, `_ps`, `_controller`, a trailing `PS`; their text differs in the controller wording, which is lost). The spike's 527 counted the 19 nameless ones. Only 10 tutorials have a picture (the nine Switch motion-pattern diagrams, paired with the `TutorialJournalMotionPattern*` tutorial of the same name, and `playstile-alert`, set on `PlaystyleDualGrip`); the other 49 pictures in the game are one identical orange "TODO" placeholder and are never used (`flat()` refuses any flat-colour picture), so those tutorials are text only.
- **Books**: 833 books (the spike's 834 counted Beledal's grandfather, whose body exists but whose title does not) + 44 paintings, maps and sketches. A book is a readable item that is not a schematic or recipe and has a body: `<key>_text`, the item id, or a one-line `item_desc_<id>` of its own (3 items); title-only items (17) and nameless ones (57) are dropped.

## Files (`glossary/` in the art folder; `tools/build.py` turns each into a hashed script)
`manifest.json` `{version, counts, files}`; `bestiary.json`, `characters.json`, `tutorial.json`, `books_index.json`, `books_book.json`, `books_note.json`, `books_quest.json`; pictures `img/<tab>/<name>.webp`, thumbnails `img/<tab>/t/<name>.webp` (64 px).
- list files: `{"tab", "groups":[...], "entries":[{id, name, group, img, thumb, stages:[text], weak:[...], note?}]}` in display order (group, then A to Z). `img`/`thumb` are `@@gimg:<path under img/, no extension>@@` tokens or null. `note` tells apart equal names ("Blood and Wine", "1 of 2"). The tutorial list has `variants` (platform variants folded), `groups` and no thumbnails.
- books: `books_index.json` has `{id, name, kind: book|note|quest|painting, group: "Books"|"Paintings & maps", img, thumb}`; the bodies are `books_<kind>.json` `{"kind", "text": {id: text}}`, loaded when a book of that kind is opened or a search needs them.
- text is the game's: `<br>`, `<i>`, `<b>`, `<s>`, `<font color="#rrggbb">` and button placeholders `<<GUI_PC_Select>>`. The page turns these into elements (never `innerHTML`); any other tag is dropped.
- `tools/build.py`: `--variant game` reads `<art-dir>/glossary` when its `manifest.json` exists; `--glossary-dir` does it for another folder (the tests, with the fake one). Each file becomes `glossary/<name>.<hash>.js` (`W3DATA.glossary_<name>`), each picture `glossary/img/<hash>.webp`, cached for a year (`_headers`); `index.html` only gets the small map of URLs (`/*@glossary*/` in `src/glossary.js`). Nothing is requested until the Glossary screen opens.

## The screen (`src/glossary.js`, `glossary.css`; top bar in `screens.js`)
Top bar: `[<] GLOSSARY ALCHEMY INVENTORY WORLD MAP QUESTS CHARACTER MEDITATION [>]`; Glossary, Inventory and Character are live, the four others are greyed ("in game only") and open a centred panel (title, "In-game feature only.", a reason, Open Inventory / Open Glossary); the arrows skip them. State `S.scr`: `char` (default), `inv`, `glo`; the link segment is `s1I` / `s1G` (appended, old links byte-identical; the sub-tab, the selection and the search are not in the link). Sub-tabs: Bestiary, Tutorial, Characters, Books, Crafting (greyed, same panel).
List (search, ALL TABS toggle, count with Collapse all / Expand all, thumbnails; the group headings of Bestiary, Tutorial, Characters and Books fold and unfold, kept while the page is open and not in the link; a search opens the groups with results; opening an entry of a folded group unfolds it) | picture on a misty backdrop | title and text. Search is case- and accent-insensitive over names and text; ALL TABS loads every file and groups results by tab with a snippet or "name match"; clicking one opens its tab and entry with the word highlighted. Phone (<= 760 px): list or entry, one at a time, with a back arrow; the sub-tabs and the top bar's tabs scroll sideways.
Tests: `tests/glcheck.py` (fake content in every build, the real counts and every real chip on the live build), `tests/test_glossary_tool.py`, link fixtures `tests/fixtures/links/v29.*`.
