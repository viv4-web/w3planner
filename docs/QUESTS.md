# Quests (v33): data, build, screen, progress

The Quests tab (key `Q`, link segment `s1Q`) lists the game's quests, shows where a save stands and lets the player mark progress by hand. Every quest fact comes from the
game's files or from the save; **no third-party guide, wiki or spreadsheet is used for data** (the credit link to ChrisRi's Optimal Quest Order Guide is a plain link, nothing of it is copied or loaded).
Spike notes with the file formats: `~/work/w3planner-art/quest-spike/FORMAT-NOTES-quests.md` (not committed).

## Making the data
`python tools/make_quests.py` (inputs read-only: `~/incoming/w3planner-journal.zip`, `~/incoming/gamedata/en.w3strings`, `~/incoming/gamedata/csv/**/*quest_levels*.csv`; `--check` compares with the files on disk).
- `data/QUESTS.json` (committed, 226 KB, loaded as a script when the Quests tab or the import dialog opens: `/*@file:QUESTS*/`): `{v, regions:[[key,label]...], quests:[{g, id, n, t, r, lv, p, hs?, o, d}]}`. `g` = the quest's journal GUID (32 hex digits: how a save names it), `id` = the journal `baseName`,
  `n` = name, `t` = main | side | contract | treasure | gwent | race | fist, `r` = region key, `lv` = level or null, `p` = pack (startup, bob, ep1, dlc0), `hs` = 1 when the Hearts of Stone XP rule applies (below), `o` = the GUIDs of the objectives with a title, in the game's order (phases by `order`, objectives by `order`), `d` = the GUIDs of the journal entries with text, in the game's order. **Quest names are the only text in the repo** (v33b): objective titles and journal text are game text and live in the art folder.
- `~/work/w3planner-art/quests/desc.json` + `manifest.json` (game text, never committed, like the Glossary): `{quest id: {"d": [journal entry, ...], "o": [objective title, ...]}}` in the order of the GUID lists of `QUESTS.json`, with the game's tags (`<br>`, `<i>`, `<b>`, `<font color>`, `<<button>>`). `tools/build.py --variant game` makes it one hashed script, `quests/desc.<hash>.js` (`W3DATA.quests_desc`), loaded when the Quests tab opens (Next up shows the current objective); the public and offline builds have none and say "Journal text is not included in this build." The tests build with an invented fake for every quest (`tests/fixtures/quests`, made by `tests/make_quests_fixture.py`, `--quests-dir`). Without it the public build shows the counts ("N objectives, their text is not included in this build") and no Next up objective text.
- **Type** (`t`): the journal's `type` (absent = Story, Chapter) is Main, Side Secondary, MonsterHunt Contracts, TreasureHunt Treasure hunts; Gwent (group `Gwint`, titles starting "Gwent:", and the card-collection quest "Collect 'Em All"), Races (titles "Race:" / "Races:") and Fistfights (titles "Fists of Fury") are told apart from the game's own group and titles (the game has no such type).
  Note: "Deserters" (`LW_Prologue_Deserter_Camps`) is a Chapter-type quest in the game's Living World group and so lands under Main (its region is White Orchard by hand).
- **Regions** (decisions by Vivek, 2026-10-17): tabs White Orchard (`world` code 4), Vizima (5), Velen (9), Novigrad (1), Skellige (2), Kaer Morhen (3), Hearts of Stone (every quest of the ep1 pack: 26), Blood and Wine (every quest of the bob pack: 83, including Envoys, Wineboys), Other & epilogue (code 8 and no code, nothing guessed). v33b: seven quests with no usable code were assigned by hand (`REGION_OF` in `tools/make_quests.py`): Following the Thread and Black Pearl to Novigrad, From a Land Far, Far Away to Skellige, Master Armorers to Velen, Deserters to White Orchard, Brothers in Arms and The Isle of Mists to Kaer Morhen. Seven stay in Other & epilogue: Collect 'Em All, Faithful Friend, Gwent: Old Pals, Gwent: Playing Innkeeps, In Ciri's Footsteps and the two Something Ends, Something Begins (`Q504`, `Q505`). "Ladies of the Wood" is `Q105 Witches` (main, Velen, level 6); the dropped E3 2014 demo entry is a different quest. Free-DLC quests take their region from their code. The code to place mapping is inferred from the quest ids (the scripts never name it).
- **Dropped** (debug, placeholder, demo; 15): the 8 quests with no title (`BidDebugQuest`, `BidDebugQuest2`, `Combat`, `Quest Tutorial 01`, `TEMP Minor Testing Def`, `Lynx Set Upgrades`, `Treasure hunt template`, `Tips & Tricks`), the group `testlevel` (`[PL] Exploration`, `[Interaction]`) and the E3 / press demo leftovers (`E3 2013`, `E3 2013 Fake`, `E3 2014`, `E3 2014 Griffin`, `MS 2014 Werewolf`).
- **Level**: `gameplay/globals/quest_levels.csv` of the base game and every DLC (`;`-separated, key = the quest `baseName`, case and surrounding spaces ignored). 397 of 401 quests have one. A level with no source is `null`: the page shows "—" with the tooltip "not computed yet", never a guess.
  Fixes, each with its game-file source: the five `plegmund_poi_01` to `_05` (Big Feet to Fill) use the one row `plegmund;40` (`dlc/bob/data/gameplay/globals/quest_levels.csv:71`); `ep1poi26b` (A Dark Legacy) uses `ep1poi26;38` (`dlc/ep1/.../quest_levels.csv:24`); the seven Wolf School scavenger hunts (`Wolf Set` 14, `Wolf Upgrades 1A` 21, `1B` 21, `2` 29, `2b` 29, `3` 34, `3b` 34) are in `dlc/dlc10/.../th1009_quest_levels_copy.csv` (the old glob missed the file). A key in two files with different levels (`mq1058 cat stash`: dlc10 15, dlc15 25) takes the canonical `quest_levels.csv`.
  No source (level stays empty): `cammerlengo` (Knight for Hire), `mq1060_armor_upgrades` (Scavenger Hunt: Forgotten Wolf School Gear Diagrams), `Q309 Novigrad Under Control 2` (Novigrad, Closed City II), `Q401 Megascope` (Disturbance).
- Six objective GUIDs appear under two quests (Q704 Starting and Q704B Fairy Land share 5, Q310 Ciri's Night Out and a Chapter 3 quest share 1): the game's files repeat them. A save marks them once; both quests show the same state.

## The XP rule (zones and tooltips)
`r4Game.ws`, `OnGiveReward` (lines 1020 to 1078; constants `gameParams.ws` 456 to 459): `lvlDiff = quest level - player level`. At `lvlDiff <= -10` the modifier is 0 and the game then gives **5 %** of the reward XP; otherwise 1.0 (Death March and Blood and Broken Bones lower it to 0.8 and 0.9: not modelled). **Only rewards whose name contains "q60"** (`StrContains(rewardNameS, "q60")`): while `lvlDiff < 10` the modifier is `1 + lvlDiff x 0.16`, at most 1.5, at least 0 (0 gives the 5 %). Every other reward, Hearts of Stone's included, uses the plain rule. `tools/make_quests.py` reads the three `rewards.xml` (base, ep1, bob) and flags (`hs`) the quests whose id prefix (`q601`, `q602`, ...) has a reward named with "q60": ten quests (q601intro, q602romance, q602wedding, q603painting, q603bank, q603getinman, q603vaultspecialist, q603diversion, q604mansion, q605finale). The Hearts of Stone side quests (`mq60xx`) and treasure hunts have no such reward in the files, so they get the plain rule. In the Level band a quest is in the hatched "5% XP" zone when its level is at most `my level - 10`, and a flagged quest shows its own percentage in the tooltip and detail (`qsXp` in `src/quests.js`). Not modelled: the New Game Plus level offset and the NG+ 50 % rule, the difficulty.

## The screen (`src/quests.js`, `quests.css`; the tab in `screens.js`)
Region sub-tabs with % done (done / quests of the region) | Next up (4 cards) | view switch **Timeline** (disabled: "Needs base-game quest data · coming next"), **Level band**, **List** | detail panel. Only the screen (`s1Q`) is in the link; region, chips, search and selection are not. Quests opens in the **List**; the view chosen last is remembered in this browser (`localStorage['w3p.quests.view']`, try/catch, not in the link) and is read before anything else on load, so the tab in the URL never resets it (v33b read it only when progress was stored too: fixed in v33c).
- **Next up**: active quests first (nearest to my level, with the current objective from the save, "not known" for a hand-marked one), then quests not started that still give full XP and have a level, closest to my level, those in the region of the first active quest first (there is no map data to measure distance), then by level.
- **List**: ordered by level (no level last), status icon, name, level; chips All, Main, Secondary, Contracts, Treasure hunts, Gwent, Races, Fistfights, Hide done; the search looks in every region.
- **Level band** (v33b): full content width. x runs from the lowest to the highest level among the quests shown in the region **and the player's level** (v33d: White Orchard's quests 1 to 6 at level 9 give an axis 1 to 9, with the line on the 9; the 5 % zone uses the same scale), every level labelled, at least 24 px per level (it scrolls sideways inside its own box when that is wider than the screen); one row per type present, at least 56 px, as tall as its content. A cell (type x level) puts its 14 px dots side by side in the level's slot and wraps to a second and third line; a cell with more shows the first ones and a "+n" chip that opens the rest ("−" folds it again). Done quests are hidden unless the band's own **Show done** toggle (above the band, off by default, `localStorage['w3p.quests.banddone']`, not in the link, separate from the List's Hide done) is on: a done quest is then the filled shape in its type colour at 35 % opacity with a small check. A white line at my level (the Level field of the bar) labelled "You · N"; the hatched zone and its "5% XP" label exist only when `my level - 10` reaches the first level of the axis, with "full XP" beside it; the zone labels sit on the top line and the line label below, so they never overlap. Hollow = not started, filled gold = active, dashed = inactive, red ring = failed, a rotated square = the Hearts of Stone rule. Dots are buttons; hover or keyboard focus shows a tooltip (name, type · region · level, status).
- **Colour and shape by type** (v33c): Main `#d8b45a`, Secondary `#9fb7d9`, Contracts `#e0707a`, Treasure hunts `#6fc29a`, Gwent `#b79be6`, Races `#5ccfd0`, Fistfights `#d2955f`, defined once as the CSS tokens `--t-main` ... `--t-fist` (top of `src/quests.css`; every use goes through the class `t-<type>` that sets `--tc`). The colour is on the list name, the selected type chip, the Next up card title, the drawer title and the band dot, and the status icon (hollow ring, ring with dot, check, ×) is drawn in it; there is no separate colour for done (a done row's name is at 60 % opacity). All seven are at least 6:1 on the panel. The band's dots are 14 px inline SVG shapes: Main diamond, Secondary circle, Contracts triangle, Treasure hunts square, Gwent hexagon, Races star, Fistfights plus; status is the fill (not started outline, active filled, failed outline with a small ×, inactive dashed); a legend row under the band shows the seven shapes with their names. The tooltip, the drawer and the list rows name the type in text, so colour is never the only cue.
- **Drawer**: in the Level band the detail is a drawer from the right (460 px, the whole screen on a phone) that opens on a dot or card click and closes with × or Escape, focus back on the dot; in the List it is a permanent column.
- **Detail**: name, "type · region · level", the XP at my level, the save's status ("No save imported" / "In your save: Active"), the current objective(s) (active objectives of the save), **My progress** (Not started, Active, Done, Failed; "Follow my save" removes the mark), the journal text (loaded on demand, the game's tags as elements, never `innerHTML`; **spoiler gate**: with a save only the entries the save has shown, found by the GUIDs of the journal entries in the save, plus a "Show full journal (spoilers)" button; with no save it starts collapsed behind that button; the choice is not kept), the objective list (titles from the lazy file) with done / active from the save. Not shown: fail conditions (the base game's quest graphs are not available here).
- Key `Q` opens the tab from any screen (not while typing, not over a dialog or the slot panel). The arrows and swipe step Glossary, Inventory, Quests, Character.

## Progress storage
`localStorage['w3p.quests.v1']` = `{v:1, manual:{questGuid: none|active|done|failed}, save:{at, file, q:{questGuid: done|active|failed|inactive}, o:{objectiveGuid: ...}, e:{journalEntryGuid: ...}}, changed}`. Every read and write is in try/catch; if storage is unavailable the screen works for the visit and the chip says "Not saved in this browser". A quest's state is its hand mark if it has one, else the save's, else Not started. The chip **Saved in this browser ▾** (top bar, on this tab): count marked, last change, **Export progress (.json)**, **Load progress file** (strictly validated: GUIDs and states only, 2 MB at most), **Reset** (asks first). Nothing is sent anywhere (the site makes no requests but GETs of its own files) and nothing about quests is in the build link.
**Import save over progress**: the review's Quests section shows the counts and, when this browser already has progress, a table (Now | Keep my hand-ticks | Replace all) and three choices: *Use save, keep my hand-ticks* (default; the save layer is replaced, hand marks stay), *Replace everything with save* (hand marks are dropped), *Cancel* (quest progress stays as it was; the rest of the import still applies). The save's journal reading is in `docs/IMPORT.md` ("The journal").

## Timeline, missables, unlock chains (v34)
Data: `data/QUEST-RULES.json` (26 KB, loaded with the Quests tab as a script, `/*@file:QUEST-RULES*/`), made by `python tools/make_quest_rules.py` from the quest-graph spike (`~/work/w3planner-art/quest-spike/v34/rules.json`, notes in `FORMAT-NOTES-v34.md`; game files only, nothing from a guide). It holds ids, codes and numbers and **no game text**: per quest `s` (story point: p Prologue, a Act I, i Isle of Mists, b Battle of Kaer Morhen, 3 Act III, e Epilogue, h Hearts of Stone, w Blood and Wine), `h` (how it was derived: c = the container of the story it sits in, g = a gate: a fact or journal state set by a quest of that story point), `c` (cutoffs: `[gate, q|o, f|d]`: the gate, whole quest or only an objective, Failure or Deactivate) and `a` (up to four predecessor quests, in this order: the phase that feeds its start, a journal state it needs, the quests that set a fact it needs; a fact setter that is a Gwent, race or fistfight quest or has a level more than 2 above the quest is dropped as a weak link; 120 quests have one); plus the 15 gates and the quests that tell a save which story section it is in.
- **Left out**: facts with "pause" or "failsafe" in the name (the spike flagged `mq3026_race_pause` / `mq3026_race_unpause`, which only toggle the Novigrad horse race while Hearts of Stone runs: 4 effects on MQ3026) and `q601_geralt_has_demon_mark` (Hearts of Stone state flag that fails an objective of Q310, not a story gate: 4 effects). Every kept cutoff is a Failure or Deactivate input reachable from the test of a fact a story quest sets (that is how the spike finds them), so none is unconfirmed. A story point the spike derived "by default (no gate found)" is stored as unknown: 191 of the 401 quests have none (210 have one: 178 by container, 32 by gate); v35 splits those 191, see the last section.
- **Gates** (the points of no return): the base game: Leaving White Orchard, Isle of Mists (`q402_escort_completed`, set in q402_ciri.w2phase block 1047), Battle of Kaer Morhen, Act III Novigrad (Q310), Act III Skellige (Q210), the Naglfar (Q501), Ciri's fate, Game finished; Blood and Wine: the Beast of Toussaint (Q701), Blood Run (Q702), Capture the Castle done (Q703), the Night of Long Fangs (Q704), Tesham Mutna done (Q704), the epilogue (Q705); Hearts of Stone: Dead Man's Party done (Q602). 52 quests carry a cutoff, 33 of them as a whole quest.
- **Which gates a save has passed** (`qsPassed`; hand marks count like the save): a gate is **passed** when a quest it names as "done" is Done, or a quest it names as "next" has an entry in the save in any state. Isle of Mists: Q402 Isle of Mists Done, or Q403 Battle of Kaer Morhen exists. Battle: Q403 Done or Q110 Postbattle exists. Leaving White Orchard: Q002 Griffin hunt Done or Q101 Agent exists (so both fixture saves, in Velen, have passed it and nothing after it). The **story section** a save is in (the "You" marker) is the highest of Prologue, Act I, Isle of Mists, Battle, Act III, Epilogue that has a quest with an entry (Q101 Agent: Act I). `ENTER` and `GATES` in the tool list every quest.
- **Timeline** (the Timeline button; the List stays the default; the choice is remembered in `w3p.quests.view`): columns Prologue, Act I, Isle of Mists, Battle of Kaer Morhen, Act III, Epilogue; rows White Orchard, Vizima, Velen, Novigrad, Skellige, Kaer Morhen, Other & epilogue; Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine as lanes laid out by quest level (the axis starts at the 4th lowest level, so a stray level-1 quest is drawn at the left edge). Gates are vertical bars with labels (orange = not passed, dashed grey = passed); "You" is a white line (the middle of the current section; in Act III after the last gate passed there; in a lane at the highest started quest). Quests use the type shape and colour of the band, status by fill; an orange ring marks an open quest with a whole-quest cutoff at a gate not passed ("fails at <gate>", also in the tooltip). Quests with an unknown story point are not drawn: a collapsed row "Placement not computed yet (n)" lists them. **Show done** is its own toggle (`w3p.quests.timedone`, off by default). A click opens the drawer.
- **Missable panel** (above Next up, only with a save): "Missable before <next gate>": the open quests (not started, active or inactive) with a whole-quest cutoff at the first gate of the base game the save has not passed (and, when the save has Blood and Wine quests, the same for Blood and Wine), with a count and a collapsed "Also lose an objective there (n)". Footnote "From the game's quest graphs".
- **Next up**: missable quests at the next gate come first (label "Fails at <gate>"), then active quests, then quests in the full-XP range.
- **Drawer**: "Story point", "Opens after" (links to up to four predecessor quests), "Fails at <gate>" (with "passed in this save" when the gate is behind the save), "Loses an objective at <gate>", or "Not missable as far as computed". A rule never changes a quest's state: Collect 'Em All stays Failed.
- **Limits**: about half of the facts the graphs test have no setter in the graphs (scenes and the spawn files are not extracted), most side quests have no story point, and a gate lists conditions on a path to the quest's first activation, not proven mandatory ones. Tests: `tests/qscheck.py` (`rules`, `timeline`, `timelinesave`, `timelinephone`).

## Missable, story point unknown (v34.3)
The Missable panel lists a quest by its cutoff, the Timeline places it by its story point, and a quest can have the first and not the second (Redania's Most Wanted: failed by the Isle of Mists cut, no story point). Those quests were only in "Placement not computed yet", so the brackets and the panel disagreed. v34.3 adds a strip under the Timeline grid (`qsMissUnknown`, `qsMissUnknownHtml` in `src/quests.js`; styles `.qsmu` in `quests.css`):
- **Who**: every quest with no story point and a whole-quest cutoff at a gate of the scope's lane (Base game: the base-game gates; Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine: their own). Today that is 8 quests, all base game (Contract: Devil by the Well; Redania's Most Wanted; Berengar's Blade; Races: Crow's Perch; Out On Your Arse!; The Nobleman and Soldier Statuettes; The Cave of Dreams), so the two expansion tabs have no strip. **Only with a save**, like the panel (it has the panel's own `QS.store.save` test); without one the strip is not there and "Placement not computed yet" keeps all 191.
- **Where**: grouped by the gate they fail at, under the Timeline's orange bracket "fails at <n> <gate>" (n = the number in the gate key). - **Look**: the same type shape and colour and status fill as the grid, 20 px; a click opens the drawer; the tooltip is the grid's. - **qsFailsAt** now returns the earliest gate in the story that a quest has not passed (it took the first in the data's order, which for The Nobleman Statuette and Reason of State was Naglfar, not the Novigrad gate the panel and bracket use).
- No new storage keys, nothing in the link. Tests: `missunknown` in `tests/qscheck.py` (the invariant: for every gate of every scope, the open quests that fail there = the ones in its bracket + the ones in its strip group, none twice, and the panel's list at the next gate = bracket + strip group).


## The unplaced quests split, and "can fail by a choice" (v35)
Both come from the v35 spike in the art folder (`~/work/w3planner-art/quest-spike/v35`, notes in `FORMAT-NOTES-v35.md`; scenes, communities and quest graphs of the base game; game files only, never committed) through `tools/make_quest_rules.py`, which reads `placements.tsv` and `choices-verified.tsv` (options `--placements`, `--choices`) and **stops hard** when a file is missing, a column is missing, a quest is missing, repeated or unknown, or the rows do not add up to the quests without a story point.
- **`u`** (in `data/QUEST-RULES.json`, on every quest with no `s`): `ungated` = the v35 placement found no story gate for the quest (157) or `unknown` = a story gate exists that we cannot read yet (34; the 3 the spike called "likely" are among them and get **no placement and no "likely" text anywhere**). 157 + 34 = 191 = the quests without a story point. All 191 are base-game quests, so the Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine scopes have no section.
- **Timeline bottom** (below the v34.3 strip, which is unchanged; replaces the collapsed row "Placement not computed yet"): **No story gate found (N)**, open by default, then **Not computed yet (N)**, collapsed. Both group by region like the grid rows (`qsSwarm` with no gate: the same markers and status fill, two lines then a "+N" chip, a click on a marker opens the drawer). Help lines: "They open when you reach the place, meet the person or find the item. The game files show no story point for them." and "The game files have a story gate for these that we can't read yet." Counts follow the scope tab (by pack: Hearts of Stone `ep1`, Blood and Wine `bob`, else Base game), **Show done** and the save: strip + No story gate + Not computed (+ the done ones hidden without Show done) = every unplaced quest of the scope. With a save the strip's quests are not repeated (v34.3 invariant). Whether a section is open is kept for the visit only (`QS.ungClosed`, `QS.unkOpen`); no storage key.
- **Drawer "Story point:"**: the point, or "no story gate found" (`ungated`), or "— not computed yet" (`unknown`). The drawer is the only place the List and the Level band show a story point.
- **`k`** (can fail by a choice): `[["q"]]` = a choice in the quest's own conversation ends it as failed; `[["q", <quest guid>]]` = it fails through a choice in that other quest. Only whole-quest failures are kept (v35 follow-up: the three objective rows are dropped, "objective closed by a story branch, not lost content"). Ids only: no scene output, fact name or text is stored or shown.
- **How the 21 candidate rows were judged** (`choices-verified.tsv`, local): each row's chain was read from the scene (the choice line the player picks, its conditions and the other lines of the same choice) through the graph to the Failure / Deactivate input. keep-quest 4 (Spooked Mare, Rough Neighborhood, Fake Papers through a choice in Bitter Harvest, Brothers In Arms: Velen through a choice in A Favor for a Friend), drop 17: the three objective rows (Get Junior, The Gangs of Novigrad, Wild at Heart: an objective closed by a story branch, not lost content) and the Safecracker rows (success route), the "Loop" output, the auction objective (playing or declining gwent both finish it), The Dwarven Document Dilemma (the failure needs an earlier confrontation and the quest may never have started), The Path of Warriors and the casino dwarves and the Practicum succubus (no player choice on the path), the two Cleaver reward variants, and the imported-save fact. Rule: unclear = drop.
- **Marker**: a small cream ring (8 px, 1.5 px border, dark halo) at the bottom right of the marker, on the grid, the strip and the two sections; in the List the same ring on the status icon plus a "by choice" tag; the Timeline legend has "Can fail by a choice". It differs from the orange bracket without colour: it sits at the bottom right of one marker, the bracket is a frame above a group, and the ring is the lightest thing on the marker. The Level band does not carry it.
