How to use the planner

Set your points, spend them in the trees, equip skills, add mutagens and mutations, then share the link.

1. Set your points

The planner starts as an empty build at level 1, with one open slot and no skill points to spend. Enter your own Level and any Bonus points (from Places of Power and similar sources). Both boxes sit in the top bar (left of the screen tabs), so they are on every screen, and also at the top of the Mutations screen. Each level after the first gives one skill point, so the total is (level − 1) + bonus points. Points available is what's left after skills and mutations, and turns red if you've spent more than you have. Reset build clears your skills and mutations but keeps your level and bonus points.

2. Spend points in the skill trees

Pick a tree with the tabs: Combat, Signs, Alchemy or General. A skill can be taken once the skills it connects to above it have points, exactly like in the game. Most skills have 3 levels, shown by the small diamonds.

  • Click a skill to select it and see its details.
  • Click it again to add a point.
  • Right-click to remove a point. You can't remove a point if another skill depends on it.
  • Hover to see the description for the current and next level, with the game's numbers.

3. Equip skills into slots

Only skills in slots are active in the game. The 12 slots unlock as your total skill points grow (at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 22, 26 and 30 points). Locked slots show a padlock; click one for a reminder of what it needs and where to add the points.

  • Drag an invested skill onto an open slot. Drag between slots to swap, or drag a skill off the panel to unequip it.
  • On a phone or tablet: tap a skill to select it, then tap an open slot.
  • Click an equipped skill to jump to it in its tree.

4. Add mutagens

Open the Mutagens tab and drag a mutagen onto one of the four diamond sockets (or select it and click the socket). Sockets unlock at 2, 9, 16 and 28 skill points. The bar at the top shows your total bonus, for example "+ Vitality +50". With the Synergy skill, matching-colour skills in the same group add extra bonus. Drag a mutagen off its socket to remove it. Besides the nine regular mutagens, the tab lists 27 special monster mutagens (three colours, nine of each); each gives the same bonus as the Lesser mutagen of its colour.

5. Research mutations

Open the mutation screen with the Mutations button, or by clicking the round emblem in the centre of the slot panel.

  • Hover a mutation to see what it does, what it costs, and which trees it allows (Combat, Signs, Alchemy). The bar at the bottom of the tooltip says what a click will do.
  • Click to research it. It needs every mutation it connects from, plus enough skill points; the cost comes out of the same points as your skills. If you're short, raise Level or Bonus points at the top of the screen.
  • Click a researched mutation to make it active, and click the active one to deactivate it. Only one mutation can be active. Double-click researches and activates in one go.
  • Right-click undoes research (or use the Undo research button, or the Delete key). You can't undo a mutation that another researched mutation depends on.
  • On a phone or tablet, tap a mutation to see its details and tap again to research or activate it. Use the Undo research button to take it back.

Researching mutations unlocks up to four extra mutation slots (after 2, 4, 8 and 12 mutations). They sit either side of the centre emblem and only accept skills matching the colours of your active mutation: red for Combat, blue for Signs, green for Alchemy. General skills can't go there. The header of the mutation screen shows how many mutagens of each colour your research needs.

6. The Inventory screen: equipment, consumables and oils

The bar at the top switches screens, like the game's: Inventory and Character (Alchemy, World Map, Quests and Meditation are greyed out: they are in-game features; click one to see why. The World Map panel links to witcher3map.com, a free fan-made interactive map). Use the tabs, the ◀ ▶ arrows, or on a phone swipe left or right. Level, Bonus points, the NG / NG+ switch (First playthrough or New Game Plus) and Copy link stay in the bar on both screens. On the Inventory screen the boxes in the middle hold the weapons (steel sword, silver sword, bolts, crossbow), the Consumables (four slots for potions and decoctions), the Bombs (two slots) and the mask; the armor is on the right (chest armor, gloves, trousers, boots), with your active sets and each item's numbers under it. The stash on the left is empty for now: it will fill when you can import a save. Click any slot and the stash is replaced by a panel with every item that fits that slot: one card per tier, a family together from Basic to Grandmaster (potions: Normal, Enhanced, Superior). Filter by set, tier, or Potions / Decoctions, search by name (a tier word works too: "enhanced griffin"), click a card to see exactly that item, then Equip. The card you wear is selected when the panel opens. For a set item, Equip set equips every piece of that set at the same tier (the nearest tier where one is missing, and it tells you). Potion slots take potions and decoctions, bomb slots only bombs. Oils are not slots: open a sword's panel and choose one in its Oil row (one oil per sword; the steel sword only offers oils that work on steel). Like the game, a piece of gear above your Level (bonus points do not count) shows its level in red and cannot be equipped; Equip set then equips only the pieces your level allows. If you lower Level afterwards, the gear stays but is marked Level too low. Right-click a slot, press Delete on it, or use its × to unequip. Set cards show the 3 and 6 piece bonuses lighting up (pieces count only if the game counts them). The numbers shown are the game's base values (toxicity, duration, charges, effect, damage), not totals; an Affected by line names the equipped skills and the active mutation that change an item (for example Adaptability for decoctions). Numbers, names, required levels and set bonuses come from the game's own data. Your gear, consumables, oils and the open screen are part of the link.

7. The Glossary

The Glossary tab, first in the bar, copies the game's Glossary: Bestiary, Tutorial, Characters and Books (Crafting is an in-game feature). Pick an entry in the list on the left (click a group heading such as Beasts, or a tutorial category such as HUD, to fold or unfold it, or use Collapse all / Expand all); its picture is in the middle and its full text on the right. Everything is shown: every entry and every stage of an entry, in the game's order, with no spoiler hiding. The Bestiary lists each monster's Susceptibility: click an oil, bomb or potion to open it in your Inventory (a potion or bomb opens its slot panel with the card selected, an oil opens the Oil row of the sword it fits); signs are shown as plain labels. Search looks in names and text, ignoring case and accents; ALL TABS searches every section at once and groups the results, and clicking one opens it with the match highlighted. On a phone tap an entry to open it full screen and use Back to return to the list. The Glossary text and pictures belong to CD PROJEKT S.A.; they are only part of the website, not of the offline copy, which says so.

8. Import a save

Click Import save in the top bar (on the website; the offline copy cannot create or import builds) and drop a Witcher 3 Next-Gen PC save (Documents\The Witcher 3\gamesaves\ManualSave_….sav; drop its .json too to see the game build and any mods). The file is read in your browser and never leaves your computer. You see what was found (level, learned skills and equipped slots, skill mutagens, gear, potions and bombs, and the stash) before anything changes, can untick sections, and your build is replaced only when you press Apply to planner. Food and items the planner does not plan are listed and skipped. Not read yet: active mutation, oils on swords and New Game Plus: set those by hand.

9. Share your build

Everything is saved in the page address as you go. Use Copy link (in the top bar, on every screen, also with the slot panel open) to share it; the link opens on the same screen you copied it from, or bookmark the page to keep it. Links open exactly the same build, including level, points, slots, mutagens, mutations, equipment, consumables, oils and the screen you had open. A link only contains your build's numbers, and the planner checks every link it opens and ignores anything that is not a valid build.

10. Use it offline (Windows PC)

A free offline copy is available on the GitHub Releases page. Extract the zip, then double-click index.html: it opens in your browser and works without internet. The offline version can open shared links (paste one into the "Open a shared link" box) but does not create them; to share a build, use the website. The offline copy uses simple placeholder artwork instead of the game's art.
